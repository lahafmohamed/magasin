# Sign-off record — design pass 2026-10-23/24

Brief: [BRIEF.md](BRIEF.md). This record states what the design team reviewed, what changed, the decisions the owner took, the verdict of each sign-off gate and what is deliberately left for later.

## Team and method

| Discipline | Role | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| Visual design | UI Designer ×2 (audit), UI Finish-Gate Reviewer (sign-off) | Visual |
| Copy and voice | Brand Guardian | Copy |
| Usability | UX Researcher + Persona Walkthrough Specialist (caissière, magasinier dépôt) | Usability |
| Accessibility and foundations | UX Architect | Accessibility floor (enforced in every gate) |

Everything was judged on the **rendered product**: a disposable database (`hitek_design_review`, built by `scripts/ci-db-setup.mjs` and seeded with an Abidjan shop's month of activity), the real backend, headless Chromium captures of every route in light and dark at 1440 / 1366 / 390 / 320 px plus one view per role, and axe-core (WCAG 2.0/2.1/2.2 A + AA) on every capture. Keyboard, focus and reflow checks were scripted against the live app.

## Owner decisions taken during the pass

| Date | Decision |
|---|---|
| 2026-10-23 | API rate limit per signed-in user (anonymous per IP); only failed logins count; ceilings in `RATE_LIMIT_MAX` / `AUTH_RATE_LIMIT_MAX`. |
| 2026-10-23 | Managers act like admin on demandes de réapprovisionnement. |
| 2026-10-23 | A payment records exactly the amount entered; deposits only through their own action. |
| 2026-10-23 | « Client comptoir » preselected for the till; cashiers and sales staff can create clients (never suppliers, no credit limit). |
| 2026-10-23 | Cancelling an unpaid invoice reverses its accounting entries and restocks its lines. |
| 2026-10-24 | A payment settles the invoice it was recorded on; oldest-first only for money without a target. One-time recompute after deploy: `POST /api/tiers/recompute-allocations` (simulate `{}`, then `{"appliquer": true}`). |
| 2026-10-24 | Dépôt staff see quantities only; purchasing pages and product creation are admin/manager. |
| 2026-10-24 | « Rembourser l’avoir » (admin/manager; espèces from the shop's open till). |

## Left for later (deliberately out of scope)

**Backend / accounting — owner decision needed** (detailed in [CLAUDE.md](../../CLAUDE.md), Known Issues, « Passe design complète »):
1. Invoice ledger lines carry `compte_id` without `compte_numero`, so sales are missing from the trial balance, P&L and account filters (production: 5 750 of 6 360 lines); a global discount posts unbalanced.
2. 202 invoices cancelled in production before this pass were never reversed nor restocked.
3. `convert_devis_to_facture()` still inserts `factures.client_id` (direct devis → invoice conversion likely 500; no screen calls it).
4. BL → invoice conversion fallback leaves due date / author empty and cost price 0.
5. `deploy/reset-demo.sh` relies on a broken seed (`seed-hitek-demo.mjs`).
6. 41 production clients hold pre-2026-07-18 deposits counted on invoices without payment rows: the recompute reports them as `ecart` and leaves them alone until reviewed.

**Minor backlog:** listed per discipline in the round-2 gate reports (visual ≈ 46, copy 5, usability 9, personas 18, accessibility 1). None blocks a task or misleads about money or stock. « AI » on company documents awaits the owner's meaning.
