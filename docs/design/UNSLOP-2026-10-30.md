# Unslop pass — audit and direction (2026-10-30)

Page-by-page audit of every authenticated screen against the *unslop* checklist (signs of AI-default UI), on the rendered product: real backend + the disposable `hitek_design_review` database, headless Chromium, 120 desktop captures (light + dark, 1440 px) and 60 phone captures (390 px), every role view. All 180 captures: 0 API error, 0 JS error, 0 axe violation — this pass is about craft, not breakage.

## Verdict: light slop → polish mode

None of the loud tells are present: no gradient, glow, gradient text, emoji, sparkle, `transition-all`, `hover:scale`, glass card or looping motion anywhere in `src/`. What remains is quieter and systemic: weight 600–700 as the default, icons that decorate, status colours spent on categories and normal states, the brand blue leaking into diagrams and identifiers, rows of equal tiles that repeat what the table says, a navy dark theme, charts painted entirely in the accent, and full stops on one-line captions. The palette (deep blue + neutrals), Inter, the layout and every feature stay.

## Direction

- **Product** — a French-language ERP that runs a computer and phone shop in Abidjan through the day: till, devis → BL → factures, stock across shop and depot, books and payroll, for the cashier, the sales floor, the depot and the manager.
- **Character** — exact, steady, plain-spoken.
- **World** — the counter's paperwork: numbered *carnets à souches*, stamped bons de livraison, the cash drawer, ballpoint-blue ink on white paper. The existing deep blue *is* that ink.
- **Moments** — (1) Dashboard: the daily revenue chart in graphite, the best day inked in blue and named, matching the « Meilleur jour » figure; the payment mix (Espèces, Wave, Orange Money, MTN Money…) as one stacked bar with its ranked list instead of seven equal tiles. (2) Caisse: the drawer as one equation in tabular figures — fonds + entrées − sorties = espèces attendues. All from data already on screen.
- **Signature** — every document number and product reference set in mono with tabular digits, like the number printed on a carnet à souches: lists, details, till, search, phone cards. Nowhere else.

**Accent rule.** Blue means *you can act on this* (buttons, links, focus, selection) plus the one inked fact in a chart. Nothing decorative is blue; no status is blue.
